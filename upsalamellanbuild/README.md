# upsalamellanbuild — Upsala GK Mellanbanan

A build directory that shares its GROUND with `upsalabuild` and differs only in
which holes are played. `heightfields.json` and `tree-cover.json` are copies of
the Stora banan's: it is the same terrain and the same woods, and a pack is
self-contained by design, so each course carries its own copy rather than
inventing a shared-asset mechanism the format does not have.

Everything here is generated by `tools/build-nine.mjs`:

    node tools/build-nine.mjs upsalabuild/mellanbanan.json
    node packages/course-pack/emit-pack.mjs upsalamellanbuild \
         apps/golf/public/courses/upsala-mellanbanan upsala-mellanbanan
    node packages/course-pack/emit-manifest.mjs

## The routing was traced first and then corrected, and the correction is the point

Mellanbanan was first routed by tracing the club's own banguide overview and
registering it to the world through three OSM pond centres (3.8 / 10.3 / 7.1 m
residuals) — `upsalabuild/trace-mellanbanan.mjs`, still committed, still worth
keeping. Published GPS hole routes then arrived independently, and comparing the
two is what made the trace trustworthy *and* found what was wrong with it:

| | holes 1–6, 9 | holes 7 and 8 |
|---|---|---|
| green centres apart | 8–37 m | 62 m and **164 m** |
| bearings apart | 0–12° | 73° and 21° |

Seven of nine agree closely — a pixel trace against real GPS, neither having
seen the other. The two that do not are **exactly the two the trace had flagged**:
their lines run under drawn canopy on the overview, so both had been forced to
due north from their per-hole sheets. That forcing was wrong, and the GPS routing
is what ships.

A second check falls out for free: every one of the nine published routes is
**13–23 m longer than its card length, all positive**. A consistent one-sided
offset like that is what a correct hole-to-route assignment looks like; a
mis-assignment would scatter.

After the card slide, the synthesised back tees land **1–23 m from the published
tee points** (median ~5 m), which the slide never used.

## The long walks are the course, not the data

Median walk green → next tee is 106 m, with 2→3 at 173 m, 5→6 at 181 m and 8→9
at 255 m. That looks wrong against a real course's 20–80 m — but the banguide
trace and the GPS routing, which disagree about where two whole holes are,
produce the **same** walk profile (166 / 198 / 212 m for those three). The long
transfers are a property of a nine woven through a large property alongside the
Stora banan. The GPS actually shortens 6→7 from 132 m to 51 m.

## The card, and a stroke index that has now been settled at the source

`upsalabuild/card-mellanbanan.json`. Lengths cross-checked hole by hole against
the club's per-hole banguide sheets.

The stroke index has two series in circulation and they disagree on **all nine
holes**; both are valid odd 1–17 permutations, so both pass every arithmetic
check this repo runs. It is settled by reading the club's own sheets, which are
images on banguider.se and are cached (gitignored) under
`geobuild/cache/banguider/`:

- hole 8 reads **"Par 4 · Index 1 / 2"**, tees 338/338/320/320/285
- hole 6 reads **"Par 5 · Index 3 / 4"**, tees 455/442/428/368/308

Both match this card's `hcp` and `t` exactly. A research pass in August 2026
attributed the *other* series to the club-linked banguide and this one to the
aggregators — that attribution is inverted, and the sheets above are why. The
club prints Index n/n+1 per hole, odd on the first loop and even on the second,
which is what a nine rated over eighteen carries.

## What is inferred

Greens, fairways and tee pads are synthesised around the routes and marked
`prov:"synth"`. The published route endpoints are **not** surveyed green centres
and are not relabelled as such. There are no bunkers, which is a gap in the
sources rather than a claim about the course.
