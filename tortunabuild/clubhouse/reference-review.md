## Orientation and observed architecture

Orientation below is inferred by matching the photographs to retained north-up national imagery and the original `way/1163533127` footprint; it is not camera-pose surveying. The main building's long axis is approximately NW–SE (314.2°/134.2° from grid north). The practice-green-facing long facade faces NE (~44°); the pond/restaurant end faces SE (~134°); the parking/flagpole end faces NW (~314°); the courtyard faces SW (~224°). Retained OSM dimensions are approximately 27.0×13.45 m, supplementary rather than measured architectural dimensions.

- **Walls and trim:** yellow/ochre vertical timber boarding, white window surrounds, white corners/fascia and a low grey base. There is a ground floor plus occupied upper level, partly within the broken roof profile. Avoid extending full-height rectangular upper walls uniformly beneath the steep roof skirts.
- **Roof:** brown/red tile; shallow upper slopes and steep lower bands on the two long sides. Both short ends have a hipped upper roof shape, leaving an upright upper end facade below. A substantial central cross-gable interrupts each long roof side; the cross-ridge runs NE–SW. Two small single-window dormers are clearly visible on the NE side, one on each side of the central gable. The new obliques also show corresponding small SW roof interruptions. Roof intersections should be clean architectural planes, guided by measured roof support, rather than a jagged laser-point TIN.
- **NE facade:** seven ground-floor window bays are visible. The central gable has three upper windows. The two small flank dormers each have one window. White boards outline the gable and dormers.
- **NW short facade:** four upper window bays are clear. Four lower bays are suggested, but central lower detail is hidden by a tree; do not claim the obscured openings as independently verified.
- **SE end and terrace:** four upper opening bays, including two full-height glazed openings interpreted as terrace doors; the second from the SW side is clearly connected to the small landing. The terrace occupies the NE portion of a low end extension and has thin metal/glass-style rails. A straight external stair runs along the SE-facing front of the extension, descending from the NE terrace corner toward the SW side. The lower restaurant/terrace lies farther SW, with a narrow red tiled awning roof and broad light retractable canopy below. Satellite dish visible near the SW corner of the upper end wall.
- **Roof hardware:** photographs show a small brick chimney at the central ridge intersection, a taller rectangular ridge stack toward the NW end, a box-shaped metal roof vent on the SE/NE slope, two light roof windows/panels nearby, and an aerial/dish near the SW cross-roof. Exact hardware roles and dimensions remain interpretations.
- **SW courtyard:** raised white-railed access platforms/porches are visible along this side in the new obliques. Full doorway/window counts and their precise projection are still obscured by roof perspective. The long detached western building `way/1163533128` clearly has yellow walls, white trim and a red tiled gable roof in official images 2657/2827; the smaller nearby hut is also yellow. These are separate objects, not extensions to fuse into the main roof. The raised courtyard access gallery is a lower structure: the photographs do not support projecting the whole main SW roof volume approximately 2 m just to cover that gallery.

## Limits and review notes

The available references now cover all four facade directions, with the SW facade least complete. Heights, pitches, roof breaks and placement should use the separate national-data measurement review. Photographs establish architectural form and visible openings, not metric accuracy; lens/perspective and roof overhangs prevent direct photo-to-footprint measurement. No complete drawings, unobstructed SW elevation or independent on-site dimensions were found. Windows hidden by trees and unseen balcony details remain uncertain.

Private `*-detail.png` crops copy original pixels without resampling; their exact source bounds/hashes are in `private-detail-crops.json` beside the cache. The 2021 club anniversary PDF was checked but supplies no useful architectural elevation, so it is excluded from dimensional evidence. WordPress media searches for `Klubben`, `DJI`, `restaurang`, `klubbhus` and `Bistro` were bounded; useful originals were selected after preview review. Existing source receipts and model files were left untouched.
