# Tortuna facilities exterior reference review — 2026-09-10

Seven existing building targets were checked against retained exterior photographs and the 2026 native 0.16 m orthophoto. The photographs support detailed appearance for the yellow entry outbuilding, the range-side hut and the main service barn. Other targets retain more limited appearance evidence. Geometry, orientation and RH2000 heights belong to [facility-geometry-evidence.json](facility-geometry-evidence.json); photographs do not establish measured dimensions.

The [reference receipt](reference-receipt.json) pins the reused originals, exact private detail crops and native raster inputs. Original media remain in the ignored cache and are not redistributed. Photograph capture dates are unknown; WordPress upload dates are separate metadata. The older photographs and 2026 orthophoto may show different conditions.

## Nearby objects retained as a backlog

The **small yellow hut** south of the entry building is separately visible in both 2827 and 2657: yellow vertical cladding, dark brown tiled gable, white corners and verge, a small front canopy/porch with white posts, a doorway and white-framed window. It is a distinct building, approximately centered at `[597446.2, 6615062.6]` in the native crop. The exact anchor and outline remain those of observation `tortuna-clubhouse-southwest-shed-roof`; the approximate photo-location aid is not new geometry. It has no confirmed function. It is not included inside the entry model or added as a new runtime object in this seven-building pass.

The **grey/brown-roofed service-yard neighbour** `way/1163780657` is the left building in the service-yard detail crop. The photograph shows red walls, five small white-framed window-like openings and a narrow central door on the southeast face. Its OSM centroid is near `[597411.74, 6615262.86]`; its separate native roof lies south of selected building 3115. It remains existing generic geometry and is a useful future facade target.

All reused photo hashes and native TIFF hashes were checked against their earlier receipts. Original-pixel crop hashes and dimensions were recorded. The reference review corrected two possible modeling errors: the entry bay belongs on the southeast long wall, and the clearly photographed grey service barn is not selected building 3115. This is source interpretation for provisional models, not a survey, licence to redistribute photos, or approval of unseen facades.
