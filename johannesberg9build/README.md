# johannesberg9build — Johannesberg Golf & CC, niohålsbanan

The club is 27 holes: an eighteen and a **full-length nine**, both by Donald
Steel. This is the nine. It shares its GROUND with `johannesbergbuild` and
differs only in which holes are played — `heightfields.json` and
`tree-cover.json` are copies of the eighteen's, because it is the same terrain
and the same woods, and a pack is self-contained by design.

Everything here is generated by `tools/build-nine.mjs` from three inputs:

| input | what it settles |
|---|---|
| `card-9.json` (in `johannesbergbuild/`) | par, stroke index and both tee columns; verified arithmetically before any geometry was fitted to it |
| `nio-routes.geojson` (in `johannesbergbuild/`) | the routing — which corridor is which hole and which way it plays |
| `johannesbergbuild/course-model.json` | the place: terrain, water, woods, roads, buildings, the clubhouse |

Regenerate with:

    node tools/build-nine.mjs johannesbergbuild/nio.json
    node packages/course-pack/emit-pack.mjs johannesberg9build \
         apps/golf/public/courses/johannesberg-9 johannesberg-9
    node packages/course-pack/emit-manifest.mjs

## Why this nine is believed, when the eighteen's own banguide could not route it

This course was **blocked for a whole session** and the block was real: the
club's banguide carries `Bana-1…18`, the eighteen only; its nine-hole page is
prose with no map or scorecard; banguider.se does not have Johannesberg;
GolfTraxx does not list the club; mScorecard 403s. It needed a routing, not a
technique.

The routing arrived as published GPS hole routes. Those are third-party
geometry, not a club survey, so they are believed only as far as four
independent facts support them — and each of these was measured *after* the
routes were placed, from data that never entered them:

1. **It lands where the property said it should.** The nine occupies
   `x -783..-252, z -880..-531`. That box was predicted from the club's own
   aerial overview months earlier, before any of this data existed, as
   "west and north-west of the manor, around `x -800..-250, z -900..-450`".
2. **It closes.** Green 9 returns to tee 1 in **26 m**, and the nine walks
   green-to-next-tee run 16–95 m, median 61 — a real course's walks. Nine lines
   that merely happened to be near each other would not do this.
3. **It clears the eighteen** by 53 m at the closest approach, so the two
   courses are not fighting for the same ground.
4. **The card slide is ordinary.** Setting each hole to its printed Gul length
   moves the tee −44.5 to +7.7 m, and the resulting back tees land **5–42 m from
   the published tee points**, which the slide never used.

No green or tee stands in water.

## What is inferred, and marked as such

Greens, fairways and tee pads are **synthesised** around the routes and carry
`prov:"synth"`. The routes say where the holes run; they do not say the outline
of a green, and the route endpoints are explicitly *not* surveyed green centres
— the source says so and this build does not relabel them. There are no
bunkers here, because nothing published shows where they are; that is a gap,
not a claim that the nine has none.
