The main roof's sampled envelope is approximately 15.4 × 12.0 m, the cross wing 8.4 × 8.9 m, the north annex 8.8 × 8.4 m and the range building 12.6 × 10.7 m. These are **inner envelopes of sampled roof returns**, not surveyed wall footprints or overhang dimensions. The JSON records measured plane support hulls, fitted ridge endpoints and rectangles in EPSG:3006 and the existing course's local XZ frame.

The point support is strong: dominant plane residual RMSE is 0.02–0.04 m. This describes fit consistency, not absolute survey accuracy. Allow approximately 0.2 m vertically when using the fitted ridge and 0.5–1 m at horizontal roof edges and ridge endpoints. No source certification of those total modelling tolerances is implied. Roof tile corrugations, eaves, gutters and sparse return spacing contribute uncertainty. The main/cross queries also capture small parts of neighbouring roof planes; their third fitted planes must not enlarge the two-plane gable envelope.

Use absolute roof elevations when placing the buildings. Around the clubhouse the sampled ground varies from about 31.6 to 32.8 m RH2000; a least-squares local ground plane does not establish an entrance threshold or floor level. Around the range building, the ground median is about 30.4 m; around the practice building it is about 30.2 m. Do not move measured laser roof coordinates to match relief-displaced orthophoto roof outlines or an inherited OSM polygon. In particular, the north annex's laser roof is about two metres west of the reviewed aerial roof envelope.

The practice building remains a specific modelling uncertainty. Its returns support a shallow or complex roof surface at approximately 33.2–34.8 m RH2000, with adjacent trees and discontinuities. The plane subsets do not form a supported convex gable. The JSON intentionally supplies no ridge or gable rectangle for it. A historical photo of a small red hut is not sufficient evidence that today's larger roof is the same structure.

The additional southern context check finds an elevated structure where the June 2024 ortho shows a white construction slab: June 2025 returns reach 36.35 m RH2000 above nearby ground around 30.7 m. This supports construction between the image and laser captures. Use and ownership are unconfirmed; do not label it a course maintenance facility.
