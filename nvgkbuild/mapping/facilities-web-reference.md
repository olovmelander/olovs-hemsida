# Norrfällsviken facilities: photographic references

Reviewed 2026-09-10. Nine curated references: eight photographs and one official visitor-map schematic. Open the [local photo and orthophoto gallery](../cache/facilities-reference/reference-gallery.html); each thumbnail links to the full downloaded image.

The strongest exterior evidence is the official course-facing clubhouse photograph: it shows the glazed cross-gable and a separate northern pavilion. Its 2020-08-19 date is inferred from the filename, not independently verified. The official range photo has matching 2025-05-28 filename and camera metadata. Public image metadata dates the historical entrance view to 2016-08-25.

The 2016 entrance hut has its own historical identity. It is not identified with the current `lm-practice-shed`. The north pavilion’s use and the exact changing-room/servicehouse footprint remain unknown. The club identifies its office, café and shop within the clubhouse; nearby marina restaurants and accommodation must not be modelled as golf-club buildings.
