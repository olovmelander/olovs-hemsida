## Architectural interpretation

### Modern clubhouse and restaurant, OSM way 530655631

`DSC4580` shows white rendered walls and intersecting gable roofs in dark blue-gray ribbed metal. The west-facing cross-wing has two upper windows and several ground-floor windows. The main restaurant wing has a larger dormer over its west/southwest face, extensive ground glazing and a polygonal glazed conservatory toward its south/southeast end. The complex therefore needs different wall/eave heights; a uniform two-storey rectangular block does not match the photographs.

Orientation is an inference from matching the building and tiny shore hut to the north-up [2026 orthophoto](../ortho/clubhouse-finish.png), not from EXIF: the two-upper-window gable is on a west-facing cross-wing immediately north of the western terrace. It is not the far northern end of the entire irregular footprint. The main restaurant ridge runs broadly NNW–SSE. The terrace is paved with square/rectangular slabs and enclosed by a low pale layered-stone retaining wall; timber refers to outdoor chairs/tables, not its surface. These observations come from the 2023 field photographs linked above.

The [official contact page](https://www.visbygk.com/besoka-kronholmen/) supplies an oblique aerial and a course-facing photograph of the lighthouse station. They corroborate overall roof massing and relative positions, although capture dates are not stated. The filename `20210615` is only a date lead. The [Commons photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Visby_GKs_klubbhus_p%C3%A5_Kronholmens_golfbana_i_V%C3%A4stergarn.jpg), Daniel Glifberg, 2 August 2009, is CC BY-SA 2.0 and shows earlier landscaping; use current orthophotos for ground layout.

### Historic station: three separate structures

The [C.H.A.B report](https://www.chab.se/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/forundersokningKronholmen.pdf) is based on July/August 2016 inspection commissioned by Visby Golfklubb. Its page 10 north-up plan identifies the small 1890 Fyrhuset, larger 1892 keeper's dwelling, 1936 tower and 1892 cellar. Page 18 reproduces the original 1892 keeper-house elevations and plan. No house heights, roof pitches or tower dimensions were found in the text; these drawings are proportion references until calibrated.

The white Fyrhuset (way 530655633) has vertical timber cladding, granite plinth, east gable entry, west gable glazed bay, dark pitched roof and brick chimney. The report describes felt roof covering in 2016. The red keeper's dwelling / old clubhouse (way 530655632) has white joinery, pitched corrugated metal roof and later glazed verandas northwest and east; the verandas' roofing is standing-seam metal. This material/history description is distinct from present condition: 2023 photographs show Fyrhuset after renovation.

The [official Fyrhuset page](https://www.visbygk.com/fyrhuset/) confirms it is lodging beside first tee and provides the current gallery. It must not be confused with the red former clubhouse. `DSC4575` shows the modern tower as a slender white circular concrete shaft with circular gallery, mainly opaque octagonal upper lantern, limited upper light openings, pyramidal cap and finial. Do not replace the lantern with a fully glazed generic lighthouse cage.

The existing scenery module cites [Fyrsällskapet's Skansudde entry](https://fyr.org/wiki/index.php/Skansudde) for 10.4 m tower height. Its page was search-visible but direct retrieval returned a verification page in this acquisition. That dimension should describe the total tower, not a 10.4 m shaft plus another lantern. Rough proportions in `DSC4575` suggest diameter near 2.5 m and shaft-to-balcony near 6.5 m if overall height is 10.4 m; these are uncalibrated photographic estimates, not survey measurements.

Full rear/east clubhouse elevations, reliable elevations of service/range/kiosk structures, and calibrated height information are still the important gaps.

## Reproduce acquisition

Run `node acquire-photo-references.mjs` to discover official page assets, `node download-selected.mjs` for the official/Commons/survey batch, then `node download-semester.mjs` for the field-report gallery. `annotate-photo-references.mjs` labels the inspected references and checks retained hashes. `build-contact-sheets.ps1` uses Windows System.Drawing to create local sheets and record raster dimensions. Retain the manifest when using these scripts: downloading evidence is separate from any right to redistribute the source pixels.
