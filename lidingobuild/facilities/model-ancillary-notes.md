# Cafe 9 and toilet 15 model

The two new facilities use the unchanged main roof polygons recorded in [ancillary-location-review.json](ancillary-location-review.json). Their locations agree across the 2025-05-31 native Lantmateriet orthophoto, club-linked course-guide amenity symbols and club descriptions. There is no matching retained OSM building ID, so both parents have `sourceBuildingIds=[]`. The facility IDs are `cafe-9` and `toilet-15`.

Cafe 9 is centred near E677391.28 N6586343.95, between green 9 and red tee 10. The roof trace is approximately 4.8 by 9.9 m. Toilet 15 is centred near E677617.13 N6586643.80, immediately east of the road and northwest of red tee 15. Its roof trace is approximately 2.9 m square. These are manual roof outlines, with uncertainty from edge interpretation, overhangs and roof displacement; they are not surveyed wall footprints.

Both models have closed pale wall masses, restrained pitched dark roofs with physical thickness, modest roof-edge caps and foundations that follow the exact published 1 m terrain at their corners. Flat floors sit 8 cm above the highest wall-corner terrain sample. Source roof vertices remain unchanged; wall outlines have a small estimated inward setback. Eave/ridge heights above the authored floor are 2.55/3.50 m for Cafe 9 and 2.25/2.75 m for toilet 15. These heights and pitches are appearance estimates without height measurement authority. The pale neutral wall colour follows visible light tonal areas; facade material and exact finish remain unresolved.

The cafe includes the small dark roof projection visible north of the main roof in `hole-10-tees.png`. Approximate native pixel corners are `(839,592), (852,590), (856,601), (841,605)`. Its shallow roof, two restrained supports and covered approach slab form a conservative interpretation of that projection. The open-canopy interpretation, support arrangement, surface finish and 2.38 m roof height are estimates. The [club anniversary history](https://www.lidingogk.se/media/xiogpfrw/lgk-90-%C3%A5r-2023-05-23.pdf) records a roof over the cafe sales area in 2015, but provides no measured canopy drawing. The exclusion outline inserts the traced projection into the main roof edge and preserves the original main polygon; it does not use a broad rectangular buffer.

No unsupported doors, windows, service hatch, signs, logos, furnishings or water fixtures are modelled. The [2022 club report](https://www.lidingogk.se/nyheter/en-julhaelsning-fraan-styrelsen-2022) places the new toilet at the existing hole 15 water tap and describes Cafe 9's refurbishment. The [current guest page](https://www.lidingogk.se/gaster/) identifies the kiosk between holes 9 and 10. The guide symbols establish the service locations without establishing facade or interior layouts.

Source paths, image hashes, native pixel traces, exact geotransforms, guide URLs and uncertainty notes are embedded in each parent evidence record. Source raster pixels remain in the local reference cache and are not used as public model textures. The module is standalone geometry code using `build(ctx)` and performs no Blender scene or application mutations itself.
