# Lidingö facilities: reviewed photographic reference

Research date: 2026-09-10. The [photo manifest](web-reference.json) selects 18
images for Blender from 52 retained photographs. The selection is based on
visual review, including close inspection of the strongest aerial, facade and
range originals. These photographs support architectural appearance and
relationships between objects; their pixels are not surveyed dimensions.

Original images, captured source pages and contact sheets are local under
`lidingobuild/cache/facilities-reference-2026-09-10/`. That directory is ignored.
`curated-contact-1.jpg` and `curated-contact-2.jpg` provide the modelling shortlist;
`old-contact-*.jpg`, `new-contact-*.jpg` and `commons-extra-contact.jpg` retain the
broader review. The existing 2026-09-07 club archive remains unchanged.

## Modelling observations

The restaurant/reception facade is off-white textured render with a pale base
strip and broad shallow dark eaves. The upper balcony is recessed behind two
blue retractable awning banks; its front has white vertical panels, rounded ends
and a slim dark rail. Round white columns carry the front edge above dark framed
reception glazing. A round white clock sits to the right. The roof is a set of
shallow stepped plates with separate rooftop service and chimney volumes.
Do not approximate all elevated returns with one continuous tent-shaped roof.

The covered balcony has a grey timber soffit, timber floor, dark framed glass
wall, lanterns, heaters and exposed awning arms. The adjoining outdoor terrace
steps down in three timber levels with connecting stairs. Black frames support
glass balustrades. Its course side has a substantial dark retaining edge; the
lower terrace is neither a flush grass patio nor part of the building footprint.

The lower pavilion has white vertical siding, ordinary framed rectangular
windows and a glazed veranda with white mullions. A broad stair descends toward
the course. The separate south annex is visible in the 2024 aerial with a shallow
dark roof, white walls, dark doors and a small projecting multiwindow porch.
The exact allocation of storage, changing, meeting and cart functions to these
footprints remains unresolved.

The range buildings need a separate appearance treatment. Reviewed barn photos
show red timber walls and dark corrugated pitched roofs, while the retained app
display currently has pale walls. Range tee facilities additionally include
small red utility huts and open dark framed canopies, individual mat bays,
asphalt, low front curbs, divider signs, ball trays and tall protective netting.
These smaller structures should not disappear into the two large barn meshes.

The 2024 aerial shows three separate clubhouse-area practice greens: inside the
courtyard, below the terrace and toward the first tee. Paths, grade differences,
the entrance wall and flagpoles help relate them to the building group. Movable
tables, umbrellas, carts and cars provide visual scale context; their counts and
positions are not permanent facility facts.

## Current uses, dated changes and unresolved proposals

The [club practice page](https://www.lidingogk.se/trana/rangen-ovningsomraden/)
identifies the clubhouse putting greens, south-range practice area, old 17th
green practice area and new upper-parking practice area. Its inconsistent bay
counts do not justify choosing a precise total. The [guests page](https://www.lidingogk.se/gaster/)
places the kiosk between holes 9 and 10, but a sufficiently clear kiosk exterior
was not acquired in this pass.

The [2026 maintenance page](https://www.lidingogk.se/banan/banchefen-informerar-2026/)
describes spring range-net work. The [course council](https://www.lidingogk.se/klubben/kommitteer-sektioner/banraadet/)
reports that the new upper-parking practice area opened on 2025-05-17.
The July 2024 aerial therefore predates that completion. The 2020 range images
also predate net changes; current net heights and pole coordinates need newer
measurement or orthophoto review.

The [club's 2023 anniversary report](https://www.lidingogk.se/media/xiogpfrw/lgk-90-%C3%A5r-2023-05-23.pdf)
(printed pages 8–9, section *Byggnaderna*) supplies more detailed dated identities:

| Club label | Reported use/change |
| --- | --- |
| B1 | Clubhouse; reception/shop/office, restaurant, kitchen and changing facilities. |
| B2 | Former caddiemaster building converted to conference and junior space in 2017. |
| B3 | Former office became bag store and tournament office; club-wash sinks at its gable. |
| B4 | Garage for 14 electric golf carts from 2017; starter hut outside from 2018; toilets and exterior washing facilities added in 2021. |
| West barn | Maintenance staff upstairs from 2016. |
| East barn | Upper floor converted for indoor golf in 2018–2019; toilets added in 2021. |
| Café 9 | Sales-area roof added in 2015; self-service conversion in 2022. |
| Parking | Upper-upper parking opened with 44 spaces in 2020 and expanded about 15 spaces in 2023; six charging spaces by the bag store in 2022. |
| Other | New toilet by 15th tee in 2023. |

These are reported conditions through 2023, not a current building survey. The
report provides no observed building-ID map linking B2/B3/B4 to the current
footprints. Keep those functions unlocated until corroborated. A local copy and
its checksum record are retained as `pages/lgk-90-ar-2023-05-23.pdf` and
`anniversary-document.json` in the ignored reference folder.

The 2023 practice-area concept, December 2024 masterplan and 2025 development
schedule remain planning evidence. Historical 1939/1941/1950s clubhouse images
were screened out of the current modelling shortlist. Commons 2020 photos are
useful dated condition evidence, clearly labelled as older than the 2024/2026
references.

## Provenance and review limits

Each manifest photo has its source URL, parent page, local path, dimensions,
byte count, SHA-256, retrieval time, date basis, visible features and limitations.
Holger Ellgaard's acquired Commons photos carry the stated CC BY-SA 4.0 license
and attribution. Club and venue photos remain local viewing/modelling references;
no raw pixels are added to the website or interpreted as a redistribution grant.

Contact sheets are only resized layouts of the source photographs. No generated
photographic content or invented missing view is included. Architectural notes
are visual observations, not measured dimensions or proof that every facility
has been found. Orthophoto/terrain registration and the facility inventory are
separate companion outputs.
