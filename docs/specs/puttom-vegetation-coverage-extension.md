# Laser trees to ±2 km on vegetation lattices of their own, CHMv2 for the far ring — revised after review

## Summary
Extend Puttom's laser-derived vegetation from the 2 km course window to the whole ±2048 m square (which both pinned COPC items already cover, east margin 524 m) by giving the ground manifest a `vegetation` block of explicit lattices — a 256 m course lattice (the 64 published tiles re-keyed), a 1024 m ring lattice of 12 tiles for the 2 m ring, and a 2048 m canopy lattice of 36 tiles carrying a calibrated CHMv2 canopy field for the far ring — instead of hanging layers on terrain lods. The review found eight defects that would each have made the design inert or the gates vacuous, and every one is folded in: publish-vegetation drops `parentId` and would silently demote the ring graph; the app decides whether to load vegetation from `ground.tiles[*].layers` only; lake tiles with no measured cell would leave holes in the coverage and collapse VEGR back to MIDR; re-keying changes every objects payload's `tileId` so byte-identity cannot be the gate; the loader's id sort would reorder instances and break the fingerprint; the per-tree overflow rule corrupts tier slot lists; build-canopy now sizes rasters from the 16 km root; and several gates named tools or thresholds that do not exist. The revised design keeps every lattice cell in the manifest (layers nullable, stands emitted wherever a campaign raster reaches), sorts loaded tiles by fixed-width ids, decides tier overflow per cell with a zero-overflow gate, hoists the coverage above SHORE so VEGR (the inscribed rectangle from the engine's own `inscribedLegacyBounds`, snapped to x −1980..1836, z −1728..2088) drives the planter, the reeds and the far ring, and replaces the density step at the coverage edge with a geometric fade whose band width follows from a per-100 m density-ratio gate rather than a number picked in advance. Budgets are derived from step-0 spans measured on the RTX 3070 over three runs, not asserted. Requests per v2 visit rise from 128 to about 188 and vegetation bytes from 725 KB to roughly 2.3 MB, both measured with CDP throttling before the step is accepted.

## Risks
- publish-vegetation has never run on the explicit-parent ring graph: until 3.3.6 lands and its ring fixture passes, any republish through it silently demotes the app to the pilot path (graphCoversHorizon false) with no gate catching it before boot.
- The coverage rectangle is only complete if every v0/v1 cell is listed and stands are emitted for tiles with zero measured cells; two 256 m cells over Lill-Rossjon are 100% water, so the old 'omit empty tiles' rule would have collapsed VEGR to MIDR and left every step-5 gate vacuous.
- build-canopy as written allocates 4 GiB per campaign against the current 16 km ground bounds and picks the manifest by directory order; the acquisition step cannot start until the manifest resolution and the lattice-sized target are in place.
- The fade band inside VEGR's edge is 600 m wide for a 16 m annulus pitch (derived from the 1.5 per-100 m ratio gate), which thins laser positions over most of the ring; the alternative (a denser far ring) multiplies far-ring instances and boot CPU and is unmeasured.
- Tier capacity caps are set from step-0 per-species maxima; if a view or flight station exceeds them the per-cell overflow rule draws that cell a tier coarser, which is exactly the visible switch the owner complained about -- the overflow gate must be run over all eighteen flights, not only the six views.
- Population roughly doubles to triples (estimates only); per-frame cost beyond ~950 m is impostors or culled cells, but boot CPU for the plan and tier build scales per instance and the step-0 spans have never been recorded on the RTX 3070.
- Requests per v2 visit rise from 128 to about 188 and bytes from 725 KB to about 2.3 MB; hidden behind boot only if the throttled wait-for-chunk-load span says so; the per-row stands fallback exists but changes the manifest again.
- A Laserdata-skog CHM counts class-1 returns, so roofs in the ring are canopy unless the OSM ring-exclusions fetch runs first; the migrated model's buildings cover only E 696851-698907 N 7025145-7026627.
- The pruner and the ring publisher walk only ground.tiles[*].layers today; without their updates a republish deletes or drops every lattice chunk silently (the surface-chunk trap again).
- The fingerprint gate of step 1 depends on fixed-width ids preserving today's tile order; a later change to the loader's sort or to the v0 origin breaks it, so the freeze test pins both.
- Lantmateriet COPC access has flipped between ready and 401; a north re-fly would move the seam and must be re-pinned deliberately.
- CHMv2 heights are compressed (slope 0.46) and its imagery predates the June 2026 laser; far-ring heights are calibrated classes, and the five felled blocks may carry trees beyond VEGR.
- The v2 fingerprint changes on purpose in step 5; the GPK1 path must stay byte-identical to step 0's --v2 off run, and any drift there means MIDR, SHORE's extent on the plain path, or the planter was touched.

## Open questions
- Should the vegetation tile schema reserve layers.crowns now for the stand-crowns study, or wait until that study confirms a per-tile chunk?
- Annulus pitch 16 m with a 600 m fade band, or 12 m with 400 m: the density-ratio gate holds for either; the choice is by looking on the RTX 3070 against the boot and instance cost of the denser ring.
- Far canopy field encoding: 1-byte class+height (est. 0.5-0.7 MB for 36 tiles) or a 2-bit presence-only variant (~150 KB) with hashed heights.
- Whether to feed the far ground tint raster from the canopy field in the same pass (out of scope here).
- Phone budget: lowQualityKeep 0.55 exists; a ring-specific LOWQ keep (0.3) is proposed but unmeasured, as is everything on a phone.
- Ring stand density: ship measured density (default) or a representative thinning inside the ring for phones only.
- The default (flagless) visit still shows the legacy planter; v2 stays opt-in until the release decision the digital-twin plan reserves.
- Environment note unrelated to this task: the claude.ai Strava MCP connector needs authorization in claude.ai connector settings before its tools can be used in any session.

Validator rules (all in `validateGroundManifest`, tested in `course-v2.node-test.mjs`):
- lattice `id` matches `^[a-z][a-z0-9]*$`, unique; `cellMetres` finite 1–65536; `columns/rows` integers 1–4096; `standCellMetres` optional, finite 0.5–256.
- tile `id` **is fixed-width**: `${lattice.id}/${column zero-padded to 2}/${row zero-padded to 2}` (`TILE_ID` at schema.mjs:22 allows it); unique across all lattices. Fixed width is what keeps the loader's `id.localeCompare` order equal to (column, row) order (issues 5, 12).
- `bounds` is derived: `minEasting = originEasting + column*cellMetres`, `maxNorthing = originNorthing − row*cellMetres`, sides `cellMetres`, equality to 1e-6; the tile lies inside `ground.bounds`; `minHeightRH2000 ≤ maxHeightRH2000`.
- `layers` exactKeys `{objects, stands, canopy}`, each nullable, each validated by `validateAssetReference(..., kind)`; **both/all null is allowed** — a listed cell with nothing to plant is "measured, nothing here", and the lattice is complete by listing, never by layer presence (issues 3, 17).
- tiles of different lattices must not overlap (their EPSG boxes are disjoint or separated by ≥ 0 with a shared edge only); a lattice may be sparse (only listed cells exist).
- `ASSET_KINDS` (schema.mjs:23) gains `'canopy'`; `V2_SUPPORTED_FEATURES` (schema.mjs:9) gains `'canopy-field-u8-v1'` (section 4).

Caps are set from step 0's per-species maxima (`V3D.treeTiers().perSpecies`, new: `TREE_LOD.tiers[s].t[i].count` per species and tier) as the next power of two ≥ 4 × the maximum over the six views and all eighteen flights; e.g. (t0 512, t1 4096, t2 16384) per species → 21,504 × 64 × 2 × 3 = 8.3 MB fixed, plus impostors 28 B × n (≈ 7 MB at 250k). **Overflow is decided per cell, never per tree**: in `updateTreeTiers()` (4096), before the move loop at 4130, `while (desired < 4 && species.t[desired].count + c.lists[s].length > species.t[desired].cap for any s) desired++`, and `TREE_LOD.stats.overflow += 1` when that fired; `treeTierMove` (4068) reads `from = species.tierOf[k]` (maintained per tree at the end of the function) instead of trusting the caller, and asserts it equals the cell state in `?lod=` debug mode. **3.4.4 The hand-over (issue 16).** A density step is a ratio, and a fade across a ratio must be geometric or some 100 m band carries most of the change. Numbers: stands plant at the field's density — measured per tile from the field, ~240–300/ha where canopy is closed (56,241 stand trees over 231 ha of 1 m canopy cells) — the far lattice at 30 m × 0.85 acceptance plants **9.4/ha** (not 39; 39/ha is the 16 m pitch). Design:
- Regimes: (i) stands inside VEGR at full field density; (ii) an **annulus** from VEGR's edge to 3 km at pitch 16 m (33/ha in closed canopy) planted from the CHMv2 field (section 4); (iii) beyond 3 km at pitch 30 m (9.4/ha) to FARR; LOWQ pitches 24/42.
- Fade band inside VEGR's edge, width `W`: stands thin by `keep` (3.4.2, geometric) and the far lattice fills the complement — inside the band a 16 m candidate is accepted with probability `(1 − keep(x,z)) × 0.85` **only where `V2_VEG_COVER.fieldAt` says measured canopy (fraction ≥ 0.15, mean ≥ 2.5 m)**, at the field's mean height × (0.9 + 0.2·hash); so positions in the band stay laser-true and only density changes. The regime (ii)→(iii) transition at 3 km is a 400 m geometric blend of acceptance.
- `W` is derived, not chosen: for a per-100 m adjacent-band ratio ≤ R, `W ≥ 100 × ln(ρ_local/ρ_far)/ln R`; with ρ_local 300, ρ_far 33, R 1.5: W ≥ 545 m → **600 m**; for the alternative 12 m annulus (59/ha): W ≥ 400 m. A tile whose ρ_local ≤ ρ_far gets `keep = 1`.
- Gate (can fail): `tools/coverage-edge-profile.mjs` (3.5) counts instances (`V3D.legacyTrees({instances:true})` + `V3D.vistaPoints()`) in 100 m bands along four transects across VEGR's edge (E, W, N, S mid-edges) and across the 3 km line, and requires every adjacent-band density ratio ≤ 1.5 wherever both bands hold canopy; plus the luminance profile (3.5). The pitch (16 vs 12 m) and `W` are then judged by looking on the RTX 3070, with the gate holding for whichever is chosen (tree-lod-plan discipline).
- What this costs in "perfect placement": inside the 600 m band the laser positions are kept but thinned toward the edge; that is a stated trade against far-ring cost, and the alternative (a denser far ring) is an open question with its cost.

## 4. Beyond the laser — a CHMv2 canopy field on the c0 lattice

**Format.** Chunk kind `canopy`, `payloadFormat: 'canopy-field-u8-v1'`, feature `canopy-field-u8-v1` (`V2_SUPPORTED_FEATURES` schema.mjs:9; `ASSET_KINDS` :23; `validateChunkHeader` :324 gains `'canopyField'` in its exactKeys with a section modelled on `standField` 466–497; `chunk-node.mjs:93` and `runtime/decode-web.mjs:136` gain an inspect branch; new `packages/course-v2/canopy-field.mjs` encode/decode/inspect). Header `canopyField: { width: 256, height: 256, cellMetres: 8, bytesPerSample: 1, channels: ['presenceClass','meanHeight'], presenceThresholdMetres: 2, heightScaleMetres: 0.5, heightCalibration: 'laser-bins-v1', presenceCalibration: 'stand-field-v1', rowOrder: 'north-to-south', columnOrder: 'west-to-east', canopyRegistryVersion: 1 }`. Byte = class (bits 0–1: 0 nodata, 1 open, 2 sparse, 3 closed) | height class << 2 (bits 2–7: calibrated mean height of the ≥ 2 m sub-samples in 0.5 m steps, 0 when class ≤ 1). Attached as `layers.canopy` on c0 tiles (bounds = the coinciding lod-3 tile's), so `collectAsset`'s bounds check works unchanged.

**Class thresholds are calibrated, not invented (issue 21).** `packages/course-geo/chmv2/calibrate-canopy-classes.mjs`: aggregate the cached `chmv2-1200130303.f32` (2048² at 1 m on the course grid) to 8 m cells → optical presence fraction (≥ 2 m); aggregate the campaign CHM rasters the same way → laser fraction; define the laser classes open/sparse/closed at fractions < 0.2 / 0.2–0.6 / ≥ 0.6 (the stand planter's own `minimumFraction` 0.15 is the open floor); choose the two optical thresholds that maximise agreement with the laser classes; write the confusion matrix, agreement and kappa to `canopy-field-evidence.json`. Gate: class agreement ≥ 0.75 and kappa ≥ 0.40 (the cross-check's own bar). Second, free check: the field's per-c0-tile canopy fraction over the course window against `canopy-evidence.json` `perTile` interiors. The impostor count is a smoke test only.

**Builder.** `packages/course-geo/chmv2/build-canopy-field.mjs --ground puttom --lattice … --cell 8 [--sub 4]`: per c0 tile and 8 m cell, a 4 × 4 sub-grid of EPSG:3006 points → `sweref99TmToLatLon` → `latLonToWebMercator` (projection.mjs:50, 94) → nearest CHMv2 pixel through `openCog`/`httpRange` (`packages/course-geo/cog/cog-reader.mjs:226, 283`, re-exported by `chmv2/cog-reader.mjs`) from tile 1200130303 or its east neighbour 1200130312; class by the calibrated thresholds; height class by the piecewise-linear inverse of the cross-check bin means (CHMv2 4.66 → 3.5 m, 6.04 → 7.65, 8.78 → 12.25, 11.48 → 16.94, 13.99 → 21.55, 16.66 → 26.16, linear below, clamped at 30 m) — valid for a cell's height class, meaningless per tree, never written into a registry. ~36 M projections ≈ 40 s; ~1,700 COG tile reads ≈ 65 MB. Writes chunks through `writeChunk` + `assetReferenceForChunk({ kind:'canopy', directory:'grounds/puttom/canopy' })`, `canopy-layers.json` for `publish-vegetation` (`--canopy <dir>`), and the evidence (both COG identities with ETag/Last-Modified/Content-Length, calibration tables, class histogram per tile, the felled blocks, attribution "Canopy height (CHMv2) © Meta and World Resources Institute, CC BY 4.0"). The imagery predates the June 2026 laser: the five `felledSinceImagery` blocks (0.57–1.75 ha) will carry trees where they lie outside VEGR; acceptable beyond 2 km and recorded.

**Runtime.** One far loop over FARR outside VEGR (plus the fade band, 3.4.4): where the field covers the point, `closed → p 0.85, sparse → p 0.35, open/nodata → skip` (no `openLand`/`fbm`/OSM-ring rule there; `CLEARINGS` and `inWater`/`isFlatWaterAt` stay); height `sy = heightAt × (0.9 + 0.2·hash) / templateHeight`; species by hash; pitch 16 m to 3 km, 30 m beyond. ## 5. Should MIDR grow?

**No.** MIDR (1523) sizes the legacy terrain ring, `SEAM = MIDR` (1548), the reed box, the tufts loop (`const GAP = 5.2;` 4337), the legacy lattice and the far ring's inset, and determines the GPK1 fingerprint, which must stay byte-identical. **VEGR** (3.4.3) is the planted ring under v2: the inscribed rectangle of the coverage snapped inward to 36 m, MIDR everywhere else. Beyond ±2048 the next laser ring needs the east item 702_70 (both halves), ~800 windows (~15 min, 1.6 GB) and would plant ground nobody sees closer than 2 km: the CHMv2 field is the right authority there.
