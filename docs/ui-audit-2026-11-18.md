# The UI audit of 18 November 2026

The owner asked for an audit of the UX, the UI, the HUDs and the menus: that
everything is easy to use, looks stunning and is built well. This followed
[the audit of 8 November](ui-audit-2026-11-08.md), so it began from a front door,
a map and a drawer that had already been through one round. The method:

- Every screen of the start menu (start menu, the three course lists, a course's page,
  Statistik empty and with its example, Inställningar) was captured at 390 x 844 and
  1440 x 900, and the start menu at eight desktop and tablet sizes from 1024 x 768 to
  1920 x 1080.
- The light map was booted at 390 x 844, 820 x 1180 (a tablet held upright) and
  1440 x 900, with its Score, Hålet and Meny sheets open.
- The detailed course and Spela were booted in this container's software renderer
  (SwiftShader, about four minutes a boot), their animation stopped once loaded, and
  captured at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844 with the drawer open.
- axe-core 4.10 (WCAG 2.1 A/AA and best practice) ran on every start-menu screen at
  both sizes, and Tab was walked through each to check every stop shows a focus ring
  and has a name.

The pictures were taken in software, not on the owner's GPU, so they establish layout,
type and contrast, not the 3D's colour or frame rate.

## What was found and fixed

### 1. The start menu's cards cut their own sentences off

**Found:** at 1440 x 900, every one of the five cards ended its sentence mid-word
("Flyg hålen, läs greenerna och mät…", "fairways, puttar oc…", "klart inna…"). The
small cards' monospace reading wrapped word by word ("Fylls i när du / spelar") over
the Statistik card's bar drawing. Between 1001 and 1240 px (a small laptop, an iPad
on its side), each card was 230-290 px wide and the arrow's 88 px gutter left the
name 130 px, so "Detaljerad / 3D", "Hemma vid / datorn" and "Lätt 3D med / GPS" broke
over two lines. At 1100 x 800, "Inställningar" ran under its own arrow.

**Done** (`styles/hub.css`):
- Where the screen is tall enough for the words (861 px and up), the three ways to
  play get a third line, and every sentence ends where it was written.
- The small cards give up their icon (the big cards carry the same ones) and give its
  room to the words. Their reading is set in the menu's own face, a third narrower
  than the monospace, so it keeps to one line.
- Below 1320 px a small card keeps to its name and its reading, as on a phone.
- From 1001 to 1240 px the arrow moves to the card's top corner and the words take
  the card's whole width. "Bäst på datorn" no longer wraps.

**Checked:** at 1024 x 768, 1100 x 800, 1180 x 820, 1240 x 900, 1280 x 720,
1366 x 768, 1440 x 900 and 1920 x 1080 no card's text is clipped and all five cards
stand whole above the fold.

### 2. The light map was a phone stretched across a desktop

**Found:** on a computer or a tablet (Lätt 3D is offered on both), every panel ran
edge to edge. At 1440 x 900 the four buttons were 350 px wide, the hole bar's ‹ and ›
sat 1,400 px apart and 700 px from the hole they step, and the Score sheet laid its
seven score keys and its card across the whole screen, with each menu row's chevron
1,400 px from its label.

**Done** (`styles/course-map.css`): wider than 760 px and taller than a phone on its
side, the hole bar, the dock and every sheet keep to one centred column of 640 px
(narrower where the green's numbers need their side). The phone layouts, the phone on
its side and Solläge are unchanged.

**Also:** the hole being scored on the card was a block of the "held" colour, which
over the dark panel read as a muddy olive square. It is now a ring in the accent over
a light tint of it, in both looks.

**Checked:** at 1440 x 900 and 820 x 1180 the hole bar and the dock share one
column, the green's numbers stand clear of it, and the sheets open inside it; at
390 x 844 nothing moved.

### 3. Statistik: contrast, a list and a sign too many

**Found** (axe and the pictures):
- The share printed inside the bar of results was white on the birdie blue (3.0:1)
  and the bogey orange (3.3:1), where WCAG asks 4.5:1 for 12 px text.
- The six tiles were a `<dl>` holding a `<span>` (an invalid list), and the putting
  bar's parts carried an `aria-label` with no role, which screen readers ignore.
- "25 % av hålen" broke between the number and its per cent sign.
- "Dina 5 senaste rundor är +3,6 slag bättre" and "+0,6 slag över par" said the sign
  twice, once as a plus and once in the word.
- The page's header (Tillbaka, the title, Exempel) stood 40 px inside the cards'
  edges on a desktop: the header kept the settings' 1200 px, the cards 1280.

**Done** (`shell/stats-model.mjs`, `shell/stats-page.js`, `styles/stats.css`):
- Each share takes whichever ink reads better on its colour (`inkOn`): near-black on
  the mid blue and orange, white on the rest. Every score colour now reaches 4.5:1,
  which `stats-model.test.mjs` holds.
- The tiles' notes are a second `<dd>`. The bar's parts are images with their names.
- Every per cent sign keeps to its number with a no-break space.
- "3,6 slag bättre", "3,6 slag sämre" and "I snitt +0,6 mot par, och +0,5 på par
  3:orna": the word or the sign, never both.
- The header lines up with the cards.

### 4. The start menu's screens as landmarks

**Found:** axe found no main landmark on the start menu, Statistik or Inställningar,
and the faded-out loading screen ("Banvy … klar") was still read out by a screen
reader after the page had loaded.

**Done:** the screen on show is the page's `main`, labelled by its own heading (only
one is ever in the tree, since the hidden ones are `hidden`), and the page-top bars
inside it are plain containers. The loading screen is `aria-hidden` once it has faded,
in the start menu and in the map. It is still only faded, never `visibility: hidden`,
since the harnesses wait for `#boot.done` to be visible.

**Checked:** axe reports no violations on any start-menu screen, the course page or
Statistik's example, at 390 and 1440 px (before: 15 rule violations on 28 elements
over the twelve). Tab reaches every
control with a visible ring and a name.

### 5. The detailed course

**Found:**
- The hole card's tee chips print the tee's name ("58") in 8 px at 62% under its
  length. A golfer calls a tee by that name, and it was a smudge.
- On a phone the quick actions (Guide, Översikt, GPS, Vy · Ljus) were 31 px tall,
  under every touch guideline, for buttons pressed mid-round.

**Done** (`index.html`; `main.js` untouched):
- The tee's name is 9.5 px at 78% (8.5 px on a phone), and the chosen tee's is whole.
- The quick actions are 38 px tall on a phone. The sheets above them (Kikaren, the
  overview, the guide) moved up the 8 px the bar grew. The hole markers already read
  the bar's box each frame, so they keep clear of it.

### 6. Spela

**Found:**
- The HUD's small capitals (`--faint`, the labels over every number) were 4.2:1 on
  its glass, at 9-10 px.
- On a touch screen three corner buttons were small: the hazard row's clear (26 px),
  the hole map's size (28 px) and the guide's close (34 px).
- During the flyover, the only word saying it can be skipped was 12 px of grey text
  laid straight over the course, usually over bright grass.

**Done** (`play/play-hud.mjs`):
- `--faint` is 5.3:1.
- On a coarse pointer those three buttons take a thumb through an invisible margin,
  without changing their look.
- The skip hint sits in the lower letterbox bar, on black, as it already did on a
  phone.

### 7. Smaller things

- The course list's facts said "Utslag 5 tees", the label and its unit in two
  languages. They now read "Par 72 · Hål 18 · Tees 5", as the other two facts do.
- On a phone, where the course list's mode switch drops its icons, the three names sit
  in the middle of their thirds, as a segmented control's do. They were pushed left.
- `home.js` said keys 1 to 4 open the cards; there are five.

## Left for the owner

**The target's label in the detailed course.** The Spellinje's card at the aim point
("Sikta här · 206 m", the club and what is left) is a sprite 30 m wide, capped at 26%
of the screen's height (`fitStrategyLabels`, 2 November) but with no floor. From the
default view on Veckefjärden's first tee at 1440 x 900 it is 105 x 22 px, with its
title about 6 px tall and its second line unreadable. A floor in the same function (for
example, never under 12% of the screen's height, the "quiet" share it already has)
would keep it a card at a distance as the cap keeps it one up close. It was not changed
here because `main.js` feeds the prepared data's source revision: an edit turns the
prepared startup data off until the next bake on the GPU.

**The hole strips.** The detailed course's hole buttons are 34 px on a phone and
29 px on a computer. They scroll sideways and have ‹ › beside them, and raising them
moves the quick actions and every sheet above them. Look at it in a round on a real
phone first, as the last audit said of the light map's HUD.

**The light map's HUD on a phone.** Still as it was: the last audit's reason stands.

```sh
node tools/check-chooser-ui.mjs http://127.0.0.1:8620
BANVY_GPU=1 node tools/check-map-field.mjs --url http://127.0.0.1:8620/
```

in a scratch folder, not gates. If the cards change again, the chooser gate's 1366 x 768
and 1440 x 900 checks still hold the fold.
