# Banvy on the course · 21 November 2026

The audit follows a golfer from choosing a course to reviewing a completed round. Lätt 3D is the primary phone experience. Desktop supports planning, score review and simulation. The original 3D renderer, GPS planner, club wheel, scorecard, shot log and backup format remain the foundations of these flows.

## Findings and acceptance checks

| Area | What could go wrong | Implemented behaviour | How to check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Account and sync | A late acknowledgement could clear a newer edit. Changing golfer could expose the previous player's local record. | Acknowledgements apply to the revisions actually sent. Local cards and pending work follow their account. The signed-in identity is checked before writes. Failed sign-out reports the failure. | Cloud and sync model tests, Worker SQLite tests |
| Restore | A bad backup or exhausted browser storage could leave a partially replaced score history. | Import validates the file before applying it. A verified multi-key write rolls back on failure. The restoring golfer can undo the last import after reloading. | Settings storage tests and Settings import, reload and undo |
| Course choice | Different layouts at one club were easy to confuse. Back or reload could discard the setup. | The map groups sibling layouts. A course link can carry tee, guide position and hole. Returning restores the list state and draft bag. Catalogue recovery keeps personal screens reachable. | Hub journeys, chooser UI and mobile map round |
| Lätt 3D | A measured target, selected club, GPS position and score could refer to different shots or holes. | Automatic guidance follows the ball. A tap sets a manual target and updates the wheel. A saved shot captures the target and club at address. A trusted finish can show the difference in review and replay. | Mobile map round, map field, shot model and replay tests |
| Two devices | Two phones editing the same live card could lose a hole or duplicate a shot. | A signed-in golfer can follow the card elsewhere. The writer explicitly offers the synced card. One device claims it, retaining the hole, tee, scores and shots. The recording phone can play offline. | Live handoff in two independent browser profiles, including loss of the offer response |
| Score review | Missing putts, a short round, or a GPS total could produce a misleading headline or bag change. | Putt summaries include coverage. Comparable scoring uses an 18-hole basis. Shot-only records remain visible alongside scored rounds. GPS totals and saved carry have distinct labels. A finished round opens its own review. | Stats history on phone and desktop, stats and share-card tests |
| Settings and offline | A saved marker could outlive the downloaded course files. | Player, display, offline and account controls have separate sections. Offline settings check local resources, show course status and link directly to repair. | Offline first visit, eviction and repair |
| Detailed 3D and Spela | Controls could move off-screen in landscape. Leaving a simulator round or practising a shot could change the wrong score. | HUD actions remain reachable on small screens, with focus restoration. The simulator can resume its checkpoint and practise from the same lie. Practice is independent of GPS history. | Play session, HUD layout and play-rule tests |

## Navigation walkthrough

We followed first-time and returning golfers from the start menu through course selection, preview, play, score entry and review. For each detour we checked the destination, the selected hole, whether a real round was written, and where focus returned.

| Journey | Finding | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find a course on a short phone | On a 360 × 608 screen, Statistik and Inställningar began below the fold. Searching for `norrfallsviken` produced no courses. | All five destinations fit on the first screen. Simulator is named explicitly, and search finds Swedish course and town names with or without accents. |
| Share a course or a saved round | Campaign parameters on a shared URL could send a phone into the detailed renderer. Back from a directly opened round skipped its course. | Shared links preserve their destination. In-app Back from a bookmarked round leads through its course to Statistik. |
| Preview hole 18 and decide to play | A fresh preview offered to log a shot on hole 1. Choosing a score could silently create a round. | Starta runda opens setup on the displayed hole. The golfer can cancel or confirm their tee and handicap. A golfer already playing can look ahead and still record on the playing hole. |
| Check a setting and return to the ball | The browser Back gesture could leave the map from an open panel. A short landscape screen cut off camera choices. | Back closes one layer and restores its control. The active card and measured target survive a menu detour. Camera choices remain reachable in either hand layout. |
| Leave and resume a round | Reaching Home from the map took four actions through Meny, Bana and Alla banor. | Meny → Till startmenyn takes two taps. Fortsätt GPS-rundan returns to the playing hole with its recorded shots. |
| Review a round and edit the bag | Returning from Min bag could jump from the expanded scorecard to the page title. The chooser could inherit a search from a different visit. | Each visit restores its own scroll position, selection and focus. Browser Back and Forward also work inside the mobile Statistik filter. |

In Detaljerad 3D, keyboard focus previously reached the close button while the menu was hidden, and choosing Banguide closed the menu without opening the guide. The drawer now uses a native dialog and opens the existing guide. In Inställningar, the Konto heading stays in view when offline-course statuses finish loading.

The repeatable journeys live in `tools/check-hub-journeys.mjs`, `tools/check-map-round.mjs` and `tools/check-stats-history.mjs`. The outdoor study below covers physical device gestures, glare and GPS reception during a real round.

## Repeat the release check

From a checkout with dependencies installed, build the root-path assets and run the local Worker journeys. The runner creates a disposable D1 and uses development login on loopback. Set `BANVY_CHROME_PATH` to the Chromium executable on the test machine.

```sh
pnpm exec vitest run
BANVY_BASE=/ pnpm --filter @banvy/golf build
node tools/check-prepared-startup.mjs --public apps/golf/dist
bash tools/check-release-journeys.sh
```

The Cloudflare workflow runs the release checks before applying remote migrations. After deployment it reads the public health response and compares the public entry script, service worker and prepared course assets with the candidate build.

## Outdoor usability study

**The outdoor study still needs to be run.** Browser checks use simulated location and a software renderer. An outdoor round can establish how quickly golfers read the HUD in sunlight, whether the suggested landing area makes sense at the ball, and how the app recovers from lock-screen time, poor reception and GPS drift.

Observe five golfers across iOS Safari and Android Chrome, including a first-time user. Use a short hole, a dogleg and an approach with a hazard. Include both installed and browser-tab use. Record the device, course, hole, GPS accuracy shown, connection, and whether the observer prompted the player. Use anonymous participant codes.

| Task | Observation |
| --- | --- |
| Choose the right layout and tee, save it offline, start a round | Wrong-layout starts, setup time, and whether the golfer can explain the offline status |
| At the ball, read distance and club, measure another landing spot, restore automatic targeting | Time to identify club and distance, mis-taps, geometry or hazard conflicts, visibility in sunlight |
| Walk to the next ball, lock and unlock the phone, log the shot and score the hole | Position reacquisition time, unexpected changes to target or club, duplicate shots, lost or wrong-hole scores |
| Lose coverage, keep score, reconnect and hand the round to a second device | Preservation of scores, shots, ownership and the final hole across both devices |
| End the round, find it on desktop, explain the putting and club summaries | Whether the golfer identifies missing data, distinguishes carry from GPS total, and finds the shot outcome unaided |

Record actual completion time, taps, backtracks, errors and comments for each attempt. Reconcile each card with the golfer's written score and shot count. Treat any lost score, duplicated shot or dual writer as a release-blocking regression. Rank other findings by the number of affected golfers, their ability to recover, and whether the problem occurs while standing over the ball. Attach each reproducible problem to a hole and device, add a regression check where practical, and repeat the affected task on the same device after the fix.
