# Club selection, course setup and in-round controls

Banvy has 15 playable layouts across 11 clubs. Bro Hof, Upsala, Johannesberg and Veckefjärden each have two. The start menu opens an activity, the catalogue groups layouts by club, and round data stays attached to the selected layout.

On phones and desktops, **Sortera** lets the golfer choose Swedish alphabetical order, nearest club or last-opened club first. On a first visit the list starts alphabetically. If the browser already has location permission, it can immediately show nearest first. Distances are measured in a straight line from the device to each club's map marker. The catalogue remembers a successful explicit sort choice. Within a club the courses appear alphabetically, with the last-opened course first when that sort is selected.

## Two journeys to check at Bro Hof

- On a phone, choose **Lätt 3D med GPS**, search for **Bro Hof** and select **The Castle Course**. The club card also offers **The Stadium Course**. Searching specifically for **Castle** opens Castle's setup in one tap. With Gul selected, Castle uses its second tee and Stadium its third.
- Preview a hole, open it in Detaljerad 3D, and return to Lätt 3D. Check the hole, tee and distance source. From Home, **Fortsätt GPS-rundan** should take the golfer directly to their playing hole. Practice in Simulator has its own checkpoint.

## Follow-up changes and acceptance cases

- **Course choice.** Search for “Castle Bro Hof” to go directly to Castle, or “Uppsala 9 hål” to find Mellanbanan. Search for “Bro Hof” to compare both layouts. Each tee shows its verified total in metres. Opening Castle setup now fetches a 4,263-byte scorecard, down from the 752,810-byte course pack. At 375 × 667 the GPS action footer measures 111 px. Download and repair controls sit in the scrollable setup.
- **During a GPS round.** Revoke location on the green and check that the display says “Senast på green”. Log a shot with a delayed position, then cancel or retry. Only one shot should appear for the original hole. Change a score in landscape and use the score sheet’s own undo control. The shot log should stay intact.
- **Simulator.** Switch between Dra and Tryck beside the swing pad. Save and leave from a settled shot, then return to the same lie and score. Finishing requires confirmation. A failed write or a newer checkpoint in another tab should leave a recoverable round on screen.
- **Navigation.** Keep a GPS round on Castle and a Simulator round on Stadium. Both should be available on Home, with GPS first on a phone and Simulator first on desktop. During a slow launch, return to the same course setup, hole and tee. In Detaljerad 3D, open the folded toolbar from its header and toggle Spellinje and Greengrid in one visit.
- **Statistics.** Open a scorecard from the last round or trend. Browser Back should restore the list, filters and focus. Share or download the example round and check that the image and filename identify it as an example.

## Physical-device acceptance

Follow the [outdoor study and observation sheet](ui-audit-2026-11-21.md#outdoor-usability-study) with five golfers on iOS Safari and Android Chrome. Include a first visit, a saved course with poor reception, phone rotation, an interrupted round and a return to the scorecard. Record the chosen layout, tee, time to the first usable hole, prompts, mis-taps and score discrepancies. Target four of five golfers completing each core task unaided. Any lost or duplicated stroke, or a resume into the wrong layout, requires a fix and repeat of that journey.

On desktop hardware, check the aiming label against bright terrain, read the last shot after the next address, and inspect a full swing and a putt using both touch and keyboard. Browser emulation covers layout and application state. Sunlight, physical GPS reception and hardware rendering still require the field and desktop sessions.
