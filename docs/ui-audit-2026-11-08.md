# The UI audit of 8 November 2026

The owner asked for a night of UI and UX work: starting a round as other golf apps do
(tee and the rest first), HUDs and menus that are never cluttered in any mode, and the
whole thing "best in class" on the phone with GPS, at the computer in the detailed
course, and in the statistics. This is what the audit found and what was done, in the
order it merged.

## 1. A course's own page before the round (#134)

**Found:** a course card launched at once. The tee, the handicap and the light were
things to find afterwards, inside a program that had just spent seconds loading. The
map had its own start card, but only after loading, and the detailed course had none.

**Done:** a card opens the course's page: posters, history, mode, tee, starting hole,
handicap or light, and one button. See [the start menu](start-menu.md#a-course-before-the-round-8-november).

## 2. The detailed course's menu and HUD (#135)

**Found:**
- **The drawer was one 2,000 px list.** It held a course card, all thirteen courses
  again (the list "Byt bana" opens), the four camera views and eleven lights again
  (also on the Vy · ljus panel and the C and L keys), twelve tool rows with a sentence
  each, and the shortcuts.
- **The course pill under the menu button printed the renderer** ("WebGPU · 1 m
  preview"): developer information on the first line a golfer reads.
- **The folded Vy · ljus panel repeated the hole card** next to it: hole, par, length
  and index.

**Done** (`shell/menu.js`, `styles/drawer.css`, `index.html`; `main.js` untouched):
- **The drawer**:
  - the course in its own poster, with its two ways out, Byt bana and Startmenyn;
  - **Spela**: the tools as two-column tiles that show whether they are on;
  - **Visning**: the camera as four tiles, the eleven lights as their own skies
    (the same swatches as Inställningar), clean view, frame cap and the developer
    switch;
  - **Mer**: Inställningar, Statistik and the keyboard shortcuts.

  The tiles click the panel's own buttons, so each tool keeps one behaviour and one
  state. The tab is remembered for the visit. The drawer's height went from 1,951 px
  to under 600.
- **The pill** says the place and the holes. The renderer's line comes back with the
  developer overlay (the drawer keeps `#hdsub` in step with it).
- **The folded panel** reads "Vy · ljus · verktyg" and keeps only what it alone says:
  how the hole plays, up or down.

**Checked:** `tools/check-chooser-ui.mjs --player` (a GPU boot) passes. The menu
lists the working keys, and keys do not steer the course behind it. Screenshots of
both tabs were taken on a desktop and a phone.

## Left as it is, on purpose

**The light map's HUD.** On a 390 x 844 phone it stacks the height strip, the six quick
views and the four tools at the foot. The quick views are play features the owner asked
for one tap away, and the HUD's fit was measured on ten phone shapes in #127. Folding
the views behind a button would cost a tap during play, and nobody has yet seen the
HUD on a real phone in a round. Look at it there before changing it.
