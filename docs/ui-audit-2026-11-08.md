# The UI audit of 8 November 2026

The owner asked for a night of UI and UX work: starting a round as other golf apps do
(tee and the rest first), HUDs and menus that are never cluttered in any mode, and the
whole thing "best in class" on the phone with GPS, at the computer in the detailed
course, and in the statistics. This is what the audit found and what was done, in the
order it merged.

## 1. A course's own page before the round (#134)

**Found:** a course card launched at once. The tee, the handicap and the light were
things to find afterwards, inside a program that had just spent seconds loading. The
map had its own start card, but only after loading, and the detailed course had none.

**Done:** a card opens the course's page: posters, history, mode, tee, starting hole,
handicap or light, and one button. See [the start menu](start-menu.md#a-course-before-the-round-8-november).

## 2. The detailed course's menu and HUD (#135)

**Found:**
- **The drawer was one 2,000 px list.** It held a course card, all thirteen courses
  again (the list "Byt bana" opens), the four camera views and eleven lights again
  (also on the Vy · ljus panel and the C and L keys), twelve tool rows with a sentence
  each, and the shortcuts.
- **The course pill under the menu button printed the renderer** ("WebGPU · 1 m
  preview"): developer information on the first line a golfer reads.
- **The folded Vy · ljus panel repeated the hole card** next to it: hole, par, length
  and index.

**Done** (`shell/menu.js`, `styles/drawer.css`, `index.html`; `main.js` untouched):
- **The drawer**:
  - the course in its own poster, with its two ways out, Byt bana and Startmenyn;
  - **Spela**: the tools as two-column tiles that show whether they are on;
  - **Visning**: the camera as four tiles, the eleven lights as their own skies
    (the same swatches as Inställningar), clean view, frame cap and the developer
    switch;
  - **Mer**: Inställningar, Statistik and the keyboard shortcuts.

  The tiles click the panel's own buttons, so each tool keeps one behaviour and one
  state. The tab is remembered for the visit. The drawer's height went from 1,951 px
  to under 600.
- **The pill** says the place and the holes. The renderer's line comes back with the
  developer overlay (the drawer keeps `#hdsub` in step with it).
- **The folded panel** reads "Vy · ljus · verktyg" and keeps only what it alone says:
  how the hole plays, up or down.

**Checked:** `tools/check-chooser-ui.mjs --player` (a GPU boot) passes. The menu
lists the working keys, and keys do not steer the course behind it. Screenshots of
both tabs were taken on a desktop and a phone.

## 3. A round under way, and hole by hole (#136)

**Found:**
- A round left open in the map (the phone locked, the app closed between holes) was
  invisible from the start menu. "Fortsätt där du slutade" offered the last course,
  not the round on it.
- Statistik had no answer to the question a golfer asks of a home course: which holes
  cost me?

**Done:**
- **The round under way** (`settings-store.mjs liveRound`). The map's own records are
  read: a round begun and not ended, touched within the map's six-hour resume window.
  The start menu then leads with a **Runda pågår** card (hole, holes played, strokes),
  which resumes the round in the map without beginning another. The course's card in
  the list says **Runda pågår** too.
- **Hål för hål** in Statistik (`stats-model.mjs perHole`). With one course chosen,
  each hole's average against its own par is drawn up or down from a par line, on one
  scale shared out by the longest bar each way. Labels go on the costliest and the
  kindest hole only. Each hole has a readout (par, average, best, putts, fairway share,
  rounds), and the card has a table view. With all courses in view, the card offers
  the courses played as one-tap choices.

**Checked:** the chooser gate seeds a round under way (the card, its resume link and
the list's badge), and on the example statistics checks the hole chart (a column a
hole, two labels, the table) at 390 and 1440. The model is unit-tested.

## 4. Keeping and sharing the record (#137)

**Found:**
- Every round, shot, the bag and the handicap live in one browser's storage only.
  Clearing site data, or a new phone, loses them all.
- A round could be shared only as a few lines of text.
- Nothing in the map led to Statistik.

**Done:**
- **Säkerhetskopia** in Inställningar → Data (`settings-store.mjs`
  `exportBackup`/`readBackup`/`restoreBackup`). **Spara fil** writes every Banvy key
  to `banvy-<day>.json`. **Läs in fil** reads one back, but first says what it holds
  (its day, its rounds, how many entries) and that same-named data here is replaced.
  Only Banvy's own keys go out and come in. Anything else is refused as "not a Banvy
  backup".
- **A round as a picture** (`shell/round-card.mjs`). A 1080 x 1350 card is drawn on a
  canvas in the app's own type: the course in its poster, the strokes and the score
  against par as the headline, putts, fairways and greens, and the card nine by nine,
  each score in its scorecard shape. It is shared as a file where the device can and
  saved where it cannot, with the round's words beside it. It is offered on every
  round in Statistik and from the map's **Dela rundan**, which falls back to the words
  alone.
- **The map leads to Statistik**: a row in its menu's Spel, and **Se statistik** on a
  finished round.

**Checked:**
- The chooser gate saves a backup, wipes the data, reads the file back, and checks
  the bag and the rounds returned after the file's own description was shown.
- The card's numbers are unit-tested, and one card from the example was drawn and
  looked at.
- The map was booted on the GPU with the new rows.

## 5. One way into a round from anywhere, and the course under your feet (#138)

**Found:**
- In the detailed course, "Byt bana" still launched a course at once. Only the start
  menu opened the course's own page.
- "Fortsätt" opened the detailed course on its default tee, not the tee last chosen
  there.
- A golfer opening the app standing on a course was shown the last course played, not
  the one under their feet.
- The detailed course's phone HUD called its 2D overview "Karta", the light map's own
  word.
- There was no way to install the app from the app.
- `tools/check-map-field.mjs` still looked for #130's removed per-card chips and the
  list at `/`, so two of its checks failed.

**Done:**
- **The list over a running course opens the course's page** (`rail.js`), as the start
  menu does. The course already running, picked in its mode, just closes the glass.
  The page brings its own control styles, and keeps every key to itself: its Escape
  closes the page, not the list behind it. A harness's `det`/`hero` and the visit's
  `q`/`gl` ride along.
- **Fortsätt** opens the detailed course on the tee last chosen there (`savedTee`).
- **At a course** (`home.js lookNear`): where the phone has already allowed its
  position (it is never asked here), a course within 5 km leads the start menu as
  **Du är vid banan / Nära dig**, and opens its page in Lätt 3D. A round under way
  still comes first.
- **Översikt** is the phone HUD's word for the 2D overview.
- **Installera** shows in the start menu's top bar where the browser offers to install,
  and never once installed (`entry.js` keeps the browser's early offer).
- **The map gate** checks the start menu's Detaljerad 3D card and the map's best-mark
  on a phone, the Lätt 3D list's card on a computer, and the near card and the near-led
  list for a golfer standing at Ängsö.

## Left as it is, on purpose

**The light map's HUD.** On a 390 x 844 phone it stacks the height strip, the six quick
views and the four tools at the foot. The quick views are play features the owner asked
for one tap away, and the HUD's fit was measured on ten phone shapes in #127. Folding
the views behind a button would cost a tap during play, and nobody has yet seen the
HUD on a real phone in a round. Look at it there before changing it.
