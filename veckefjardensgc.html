Mästerskapsbanan

Norrland's highest-ranked golf course sits on the shore of Veckefjärden, two kilometres south of Örnsköldsvik in the Höga Kusten region. Kent and Peter Forsberg drove the project; ground was broken in 1999 and the course opened for play in 2003 to a design by Tommy Nordström. It is a heath-and-parkland layout of two returning nines, and in 2020 Svensk Golf placed it among Sweden's fifty best courses.

How accurate is this model?

Exact: every hole's par, stroke index and all six sets of tee distances come straight from the club's own card, and each hole in this model is built to its true championship length. The hazards are placed on the sides the club's hole guide describes — bunkers left on 3, ditch right; water left and out of bounds right on 11; the peninsula green on 14; the brook across the second shot on 18, and so on.

Interpreted: the club publishes no survey data, and its GPS hole maps are locked behind a script, so the routing here is a reconstruction — a compact two-returning-nines loop solved to hit all eighteen real distances while keeping playing corridors safely apart. Green contours, tree lines and the shape of the shoreline are informed invention, not survey. Treat it as a faithful sketch, not a substitute for the real thing.
