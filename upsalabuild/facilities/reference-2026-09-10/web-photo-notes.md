## The 2026 Halfway House

[Nytt servicehus](https://upsalagk.se/news/nytt-servicehus-2026/) announced demolition of the old kiosk/WC building starting 3 March 2026 and a replacement of about 46 m². The [soft-opening report](https://upsalagk.se/news/halfway-house-smygoppnat-2026/) confirms opening on 14 May 2026. The [formal inauguration announcement](https://upsalagk.se/news/invigning-14e-juni-2026/) specifies 14 June and says the new building occupies the former café site. These describe one project. The June 2025 LM imagery predates it.

The three official drawings show a proposed **7.2 × 6.4 m** plan with café, service counter and two toilets, plus labeled north/south/east/west elevations. The elevations depict a shallow gable roof, vertical cladding and extensive glazing on one long face. No readable height or roof-angle dimensions, georeferenced site plan, or higher-resolution linked PDF were found. Compass labels belong to the design and have not been verified against the completed building.

The fourth image is a marketing visualization with a flat-looking roof/fascia, inconsistent with the gabled elevations. It is retained solely as labeled design context and must not supply as-built geometry or materials. The opening photograph is a real **interior**, showing a sloping pale ceiling and wood-toned counter/cabinetry; it does not establish the exterior or mapped footprint.

**The small dark-roof pavilion on the clubhouse terrace is not established as this new Halfway House.** It already appears in the terrace photograph uploaded in July 2025, before the announced replacement. Preserve it as a separate, function-unverified pavilion. A bounded search of official news, public media from March–June 2026, restaurant pages and exterior-image search did not find a verified as-built Halfway House exterior.

Hotel-bedroom teasers, food photographs, portraits, course-only photographs and illustrated course maps were screened out.
