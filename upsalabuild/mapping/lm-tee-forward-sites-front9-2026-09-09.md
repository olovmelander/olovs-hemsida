# Stora front-nine forward tee follow-up, 9 September 2026

The club-linked hole guides identify the five remaining tee-42 sites on holes
3, 4, 5, 7 and 8 as fairway tees. Their lack of a separate platform is supported
by published site evidence. The guides are illustrations without a geographic
grid, so they do not verify the model's exact point coordinates.

Hole 6 revealed a concrete omission: its fourth platform lies across the path
from the other three decks. The guide assigns 51, 47 and 42 to this same site.
The earlier review incorrectly associated 51 with the third platform; it left
47 and 42 beside the path. That association is superseded by the new physical
platform and reference ledgers.

The H6 oval measures 73.673 m² in the declared local frame. Its centroid is
approximately `[-194.554, 486.924]`. June 2025 native Lantmäteriet imagery exposes
the east and south edge. The municipal 2017 archive exposes the north and west
edge hidden by present canopy; a 2023 image corroborates the site but is partly
shadowed. The accepted outline has 2.5 m interpretation uncertainty. Archive
service years are not exact capture dates, and absolute horizontal accuracy is
unknown. Exact grid transforms, image hashes, manual pixel vertices, source
segments and projected/local rings are archived in
[the physical platform ledger](lm-stora-tee-platform-followup-2026-09-09.json).

[The follow-up reference ledger](lm-tee-followup-stora-2026-09-09.json) pins the
post-correction model's original H6 marks, routes and card distances. Its helper
check moves 51 by 30.334 m and 47/42 by 18.771 m, keeps the other references and
all prior platforms, and leaves every route/card/other hole unchanged. The
resulting three points were visually inspected on the source image and lie on
visible short-cut grass. They are navigation references, not surveyed daily
marker positions.

A subsequent back-nine cross-check found the same omission on H18. Its guide
shows a fourth, forward round platform for 51/47/42. The full 79.997 m² oval is
visible beside the curving path in native June 2025 imagery. It is added by the
same ledgers with 1 m boundary interpretation uncertainty; all three references
move to the added platform. H18 guide and image evidence are recorded there.
